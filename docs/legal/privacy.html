Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026 · covers Nexus Command Center, LimeLog and StudyDesk

This policy covers all three Limecore apps together, because they share one account system and one database. It describes what the apps actually do — if anything here does not match the software, that is a bug, and we want to hear about it.

The short version.
  • Without an account, nothing leaves your device. All three apps work fully offline as a guest.
  • With an account, your data syncs so you can use more than one device and survive changing phone.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDKs. We do not know which screens you opened or for how long.
  • The AI features are off until you switch them on in Settings, in every app.
  • You can export everything and delete your account and all its data from inside StudyDesk, without asking us. In the other two apps, ask us and we will do it.
  • One account works across all three apps. Deleting it deletes everything, in all three.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Limecore Studio, a sole trader based in Helsinki, Finland.

Contact for anything in this policy, including data requests: l1m3core@gmail.com. We answer within one calendar month, and usually much sooner.

Guest mode

Every app can be used without an account. In guest mode everything you enter — your finances, training, coursework, tasks and habits — is written to your device's local storage and nowhere else. We never receive it, cannot read it, and cannot recover it for you. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

One honest caveat: Nexus Command Center still fetches public market prices and exchange rates while you are a guest, because a price chart with no prices is not much use. Those requests carry a ticker symbol, never anything you entered. See Who else sees your data.

What is stored when you sign in

Your data lives in a Supabase database hosted in Stockholm, Sweden (EU). Every table enforces row-level security, so the database itself — not just the app code — prevents anyone from reading rows that are not theirs.

No location, no contacts, no advertising or device identifiers, no browsing history, no usage analytics. The apps request no permissions for any of it.

Google

  • The AI features, described in full below.

Market data providers (Nexus Command Center only)

To show prices, exchange rates and market indicators, Nexus Command Center requests public data from CoinGecko, Yahoo Finance, Finnhub, open.er-api.com, the European Central Bank, the US Federal Reserve, NYSE and CNN's market-indicator feed.

These requests carry a ticker symbol or a currency pair and nothing else — no account, no name, no email, no holdings, no amounts. They cannot see what you own or how much. They do, like any web request, see the IP address it came from. They happen whether or not you have an account, because prices are the same for everyone. The other two apps make no such requests.

The AI features

Each app has one optional feature that sends something to Google's Gemini model. All three are off until you switch them on in Settings, and they stay off after a fresh install, after a reinstall, and for anyone who signs in on a device where someone else turned them on.

StudyDesk — study debriefSends the note you type after a study session. Gemini returns the topic, a 1–5 comprehension rating, anything you flagged as confusing, and a one-line summary.
LimeLog — workout debriefSends the note you type after a session. Gemini returns an effort rating, any body parts you mentioned as sore, a mood, and a one-line summary.
Nexus Command Center — weekly summarySends your domain scores only — five numbers between 0 and 100, plus the pattern headlines already shown on the Life tab. It never sends transactions, amounts, account names, task text or habit names. Gemini returns two or three sentences.
Turning the switch on is how you consent, and turning it off is how you withdraw. While it is off, nothing whatsoever is sent to Gemini and every other feature works identically. While it is on, please do not write anything in those notes you would not want processed by a third party.

What is sent may be processed outside the EEA, under the Standard Contractual Clauses Google offers for international transfers. We do not send your email address, your name or your account ID along with it.

Why we are allowed to store it

  • To provide the app you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — your finances, training, coursework, tasks and habits. Without them there is no app.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — the AI features. You give it by switching them on in Settings, and withdraw it by switching them off. To remove what was already sent, delete the sessions those notes belong to, or delete your account.

Age

You must be at least 16 to create an account, or the minimum age for consenting to online services where you live if that is lower — it is 13 in some EU countries.

StudyDesk in particular is used by people at school. If you are under that age, you can still use any of these apps: use them as a guest, where no account exists and nothing leaves your device. If you believe someone under that age has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

  • Until you delete it. We do not expire accounts or quietly remove old data.
  • When you delete an item in an app, it is marked deleted and hidden immediately, and the marker is kept so the deletion reaches your other devices. Those markers are removed from the database when you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, everything is erased immediately and irreversibly, across all three apps. There is no recovery window. Encrypted infrastructure backups roll off within 7 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, and receive it in a portable format.

Two of those are buttons rather than requests, so you never have to wait for us:

  • Export my data — downloads everything we hold, as JSON.
  • Delete my account — erases the account and all its data, across all three apps.
Both buttons are in StudyDesk's Settings today. Nexus Command Center and LimeLog have not got them yet — we are adding them. Because all three apps share one account, running either button in StudyDesk covers your data in all three. If you do not use StudyDesk, email l1m3core@gmail.com and we will do it for you; we answer within one calendar month and normally within days.

For anything else, email l1m3core@gmail.com.

If you think we have handled your data badly you can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi), or to the supervisory authority where you live.

Security

Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth; we never see or store your password. All traffic is HTTPS. Every table has row-level security enabled, enforced by the database rather than by application code, so a bug in an app cannot expose one user's rows to another.

No system is perfect. If we discover a breach affecting your personal data we will notify the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman within 72 hours and tell you directly where the law requires it.
