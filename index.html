How these apps work

  • Local-first. Everything works offline. Cloud sync is optional and off until you sign in.
  • No tracking. No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • Your data is yours. Export it or delete it from inside the app, whenever you like.
  • Open source. MIT licensed. Read exactly what the apps send, and when.
